# Information required from you (for the two WeChat class application documents)

Fill in the placeholders in the two templates. You need to provide:

--- 
1. COMPANY (for both documents)
   - [Company legal name]          e.g. Xituan Pty Ltd
   - [ACN]                          (9 digits, from ASIC)
   - [ABN]                          (11 digits, from ATO/ABR)
   - [Registered address]           full address as on ASIC/ABR
   - [Optional: letterhead]         if you have a logo/letterhead, use it when printing to sign

---
2. SIGNATORY (person who will sign both documents)
   - [Full name]                    e.g. Zhang San
   - [Title]                        e.g. Director, or Company Secretary
   - [Email]                        platform contact email (for Consumer Protection Policy – support contact)

---
3. PLATFORM (optional, for tailoring the Consumer Protection Policy)
   - [Platform / app name]          e.g. Xituan Mini Program, or your brand name
   - [Support email]                for customer complaints/disputes (can be same as above)
   - [Change-of-mind refunds]       Yes or No – does your platform allow refunds when the customer simply changes mind? (e.g. "Yes, within 7 days if unopened")

---
4. YOU ALREADY HAVE (you obtain these yourself, not from us)
   - ASIC: Certificate of Registration or current company extract (download from ASIC)
   - ABN: ABN Lookup result for your company (screenshot or print from abr.business.gov.au)

After you provide the above, replace the placeholders in:
  - wechat-ecommerce-platform-qualification-letter.txt
  - wechat-consumer-protection-policy.txt
then print on company letterhead (or plain with company name/address at top), sign and date, and upload as required.
