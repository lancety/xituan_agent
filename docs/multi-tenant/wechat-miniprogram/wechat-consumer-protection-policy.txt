Consumer Protection Policy

1. Introduction

Xituan Pty Ltd ("we", "our", "the Platform") operates Xituan, an e-commerce platform for local merchants in Australia. This Consumer Protection Policy describes how we protect consumers and handle refunds and disputes. We and our merchants comply with the Australian Consumer Law (ACL) and other applicable laws.

2. Consumer guarantees

Under the Australian Consumer Law, consumers are entitled to remedies (repair, replacement, or refund) when goods or services do not meet consumer guarantees – for example, when goods are faulty, not as described, or not fit for purpose. Our platform and merchants do not exclude these rights.

3. Refunds and returns

- Faulty goods or failed services: You may be entitled to a refund, replacement, or repair. Contact the merchant first. If the issue is not resolved, contact us at service@xituan.com.au.
- Non-faulty / change of mind: Food products are not eligible for change-of-mind refunds. For other products, whether change-of-mind refunds or returns are available depends on each merchant's specific rules. Please check the merchant's listing or contact the merchant or us at service@xituan.com.au.
- Non-delivery: If you did not receive the goods, contact the merchant first, then us at service@xituan.com.au if unresolved.

4. Dispute resolution

Step 1: Contact the merchant directly (order details or merchant contact are in your order/account).
Step 2: If the matter is not resolved, contact our support at service@xituan.com.au with your order reference and a short description. We will assist in coordinating between you and the merchant.
Step 3: We do not replace the merchant's legal responsibility for their goods or services; we facilitate resolution where possible. You may also contact your state/territory consumer agency (e.g. NSW Fair Trading) for advice or complaint.

5. Risk notice

- Transactions on the platform are between you and the merchant. The platform provides the marketplace; we do not guarantee the quality of individual goods or services beyond our obligations under the ACL and our policies.
- Payments are processed through Airwallex, a licensed payment provider. Merchant funds are held and managed by Airwallex under its regulatory framework, with segregated accounts per merchant; the platform does not hold or control your funds. Please keep records of your orders and communications.

6. Contact

For consumer protection and dispute enquiries:
Email: service@xituan.com.au
